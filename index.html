I'm Courtney, an IB PYP homeroom teacher in China. Over eight years abroad I've learned a lot about what makes international teaching work, and a lot about what wastes a teacher's time.

Everything here started as something I built for my own planning or my own move abroad. If it saved me hours, I tidy it up and share it, so you can spend less time on paperwork and more time with your students.
